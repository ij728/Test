# Super Bros Adventure 🍄

A Mario-style platformer starring your two favorite heroes — playable right on your phone!

## How to play on your mobile

The whole game is a single file: `index.html`. Easiest ways to play:

1. **GitHub Pages (recommended):** In this repo go to *Settings → Pages*, set the source to this branch, and open the link GitHub gives you on your phone.
2. **Direct download:** Download `index.html` to your phone and open it in any browser (Safari/Chrome). No internet needed after that.

Tip: rotate your phone to **landscape** for the best experience.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| ◀ / ▶ buttons (or left/right arrow keys) | Move |
| **A** button (or Space / ↑) | Jump — hold for a higher jump |

## Goal

- 🪙 Grab coins (+10 points)
- ❓ Bonk ?-blocks from below for hidden coins
- 👾 Stomp enemies from above (+100) — but don't touch them from the side!
- ⛳ Reach the flag and the castle to win (+1000)
- You have 3 lives. Watch out for pits!

## Characters

Pick **Marvel Kid** (red shirt) or **LA Kid** (blue jersey) on the start screen — both faces come from your photo, embedded right into the game.
